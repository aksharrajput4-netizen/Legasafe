# Legasafe 🛡️
### Know Where Your Money Goes

Free Indian bank statement analyser — detects forgotten subscriptions, categorises UPI spending, and shows your financial health score. **Your data never leaves your device.**

🔗 **[Try the live app →](https://legasafe.streamlit.app)**
🌐 **[Landing page →](https://aksharrajput4-netizen.github.io/Legasafe)**

---

## Features
- 🔍 **Subscription detector** — finds recurring charges you forgot about
- 📊 **UPI categorisation** — food, shopping, bills, transfers
- 💯 **Financial health score** — instant snapshot of your spending
- 📈 **Monthly trends** — visualise where money goes over time
- 🚨 **Budget alerts** — get warned before you overspend
- 📥 **CSV export** — download your data anytime

---

## How it works
1. Download your bank statement as PDF (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak supported)
2. Upload it to Legasafe
3. Get instant insights — subscriptions, categories, health score
4. Export to CSV if you want

**No login. No server upload. 100% private.**
