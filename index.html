Does Legasafe work with SBI bank statements?

Yes. Legasafe works with SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, and most major Indian bank PDF statements. Download your statement from your bank app or net banking and upload it directly.

Is my bank statement data safe with Legasafe?

Completely. Legasafe processes your PDF entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server. When you close the tab, everything is gone.

How do I find forgotten subscriptions in my bank statement?

Upload your bank statement PDF and Legasafe automatically scans for recurring charges — Netflix, Hotstar, Spotify, Zomato Gold, Amazon Prime, and 50+ other Indian services.

Does Legasafe understand UPI transactions?

Yes. Legasafe categorises UPI payments automatically — including informal merchant names like "Sharma Medical" or "Bittu Tiki Wala". Every UPI rupee gets a spending category.

Can I export my data from Legasafe?

Yes. Download your full transaction list, subscriptions breakdown, and summary report as CSV files — works in Excel, Google Sheets, or any budgeting tool.

Is Legasafe free to use?

Yes, completely free. No account required, no credit card, no hidden charges. Upload your PDF and get results instantly.

SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Bank of Baroda, Punjab National Bank, Canara Bank, and most other scheduled banks that provide PDF statements.
