Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Legasafe.

Does Legasafe work with SBI bank statements?

Yes. Legasafe works with SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, and most major Indian bank PDF statements. Download your statement from your bank's app or net banking portal and upload it directly. No special format required.

Is my bank statement data safe with Legasafe?

Completely. Legasafe processes your PDF entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Your financial data never leaves your phone or computer. When you close the tab, everything is gone.

How do I find forgotten subscriptions in my bank statement?

Upload your bank statement PDF to Legasafe and it automatically scans for recurring charges — Netflix, Hotstar, Spotify, Zomato Gold, Amazon Prime, and 50+ other Indian services. It also catches annual subscriptions and flags charges you might have forgotten about.

Does Legasafe understand UPI transactions?

Yes. Legasafe categorises UPI payments automatically — including informal merchant names like "Sharma Medical" or "Bittu Tiki Wala". Every UPI rupee gets a spending category so you can see exactly where your money goes.

Can I export my transaction data from Legasafe?

Yes. You can download your full transaction list, subscriptions breakdown, and summary report as CSV files. Use it in Excel, Google Sheets, or any budgeting app you prefer.

Is Legasafe free to use?

Yes, completely free. No account required, no credit card, no hidden charges. Just upload your PDF and get your results instantly.

What is a financial health score?

Legasafe analyses your spending patterns — subscription load, savings rate, spending spikes — and gives you a score out of 100. It's a quick snapshot of whether your finances are on track this month.

Which Indian banks does Legasafe support?

Legasafe supports all major Indian banks including SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Bank of Baroda, Punjab National Bank, Canara Bank, and most other scheduled banks. If your bank provides a PDF statement, Legasafe can read it.
